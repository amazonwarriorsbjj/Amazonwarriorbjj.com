What is Jiu-Jitsu?

Leverage over strength

Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu is a grappling martial art that teaches how to control distance, use leverage, and work toward safer positions — including against a larger or stronger opponent.

Training covers movement, escapes, positional control, takedowns, and submissions, practiced with a partner under close supervision.

Why train?

What you get on the mat

  • Strength, coordination, and real fitness.
  • Focus, patience, and problem-solving under pressure.
  • Confidence through progress you can feel.
  • Respect, teamwork, and a team that has your back.
